Architecture

Six Layers. One Continuous Flow.

Most businesses have software that stores data. We build an OS that processes intelligence. Here is how the Business AI OS is structured.

People Layer
The highest level of the stack. Humans set the strategic direction, define guardrails, and handle the exceptions that AI cannot resolve.
Applications Layer
The tools you already use (ERPNext, WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack). We connect these rather than replacing them, creating a unified interface.
Data Layer
A single, immutable record of truth. No more silos. Every transaction, message, and event is recorded and available for the AI Core.
Automation Layer
Deterministic, rule-based workflows. These handle the "if-this-then-that" logic that must happen exactly the same way every time.
AI Agent Layer
Specialized agents that reason, decide, and act. They don't just follow rules; they achieve goals within the guardrails set by the People Layer.
Business AI OS Core
The central intelligence engine. It reads the Data Layer, coordinates the Agents, and ensures the entire organization learns from every action.

Why this matters?

In traditional software, data is trapped in the Applications Layer. You have to manually move it to make a decision. In the Business AI OS, data flows downward to the core, and decisions flow upward to the agents, happening in milliseconds.
